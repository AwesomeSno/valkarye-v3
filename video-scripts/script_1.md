## Clean Spoken Script (Spoken in Video)

> "Hey everyone. A few years back, I made a robotic arm just for fun, and today I'm finally bringing it back to life as Valkyrie V3. I kind of lost my old one, so I'm trying to remake it, make it even better so that it can actually move objects right now and respond to voice commands and let you control every single axis of it with an app on your phone or desktop.
>
> Here's what I've done so far:
> - Laid out the core architecture
> - Planned the multi-axial servo configuration
> - Designed the wireless control pipeline for both companion app and the voice recognition
>
> What I'm going to do next is list out the materials which I need to build this entire thing, model the joints of the arm in CAD, and start testing the motor drivers and firmware.
>
> Thank you for listening. See you in the next one!"
